Privacy policy

Water Cymatics, published by CymaWare. 23 September 2026.

Water Cymatics runs on your Windows PC. It does not have an account, and it does not send what you do to CymaWare or to anyone else.

Sound

If you choose PC sound, the app reads the audio already playing through your speakers so the dish can follow the pitch and loudness. That audio is used on this computer while the window is open. It is not recorded, not saved, and not transmitted.

The built-in tone is generated on this computer.

Settings

Sliders, colours, and the other controls are saved on this PC so they are still there the next time you open the app. They stay on this PC.

Purchases

If you buy the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the payment. CymaWare does not receive your card number or other payment details.

What this app does not do

It does not show ads, it does not include analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information. There is no sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact on the Water Cymatics listing in the Microsoft Store.
